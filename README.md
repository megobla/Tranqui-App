# Brand overview
A yoga Companion Microapp for an independent yoga instructor with an established community of students who trust her method and experience.

# The problem
Her students find relief in class, but stress doesn’t wait for the next session. In moments of anxiety, overwhelm, or restlessness at home, at work, or in the middle of the night, they have no reliable, trusted resource to turn to.

How might we extend the calm of the yoga studio into her students’ everyday moments of stress?”

# The solution
A web app that works as an extension of the studio: a simple, calming toolkit her clients can open anytime to regain calm, built entirely on the instructor’s own practice and guidance.


# Target Audience
Venezuelan women aged 30-60 (about 90% of her clientele), in two main groups:
- Overloaded caregivers and professionals: managing work, family, and household responsibilities under constant stress.
- Retired or semi-retired women: looking for a calming, structured activity to invest time in.

# Objectives
- Let users reach calm in 2 taps or fewer from opening the app.
- Continuity: to keep students connected to her method between classes.
- Authenticity: every resource is created by her, so it feels like her voice, not a generic wellness product.
- Business value: strengthens loyalty to the studio and adds a differentiating service to her offering.

# Core features
- Guided breathing exercises for quick relief.
- Relaxing music: curated tracks.
- Ambient sounds: rain, birds, sea, and similar soundscapes.
- Mood-based tips: advice tailored to how the user feels (stress, anxiety, etc.), so the app responds to the moment instead of making users search.
